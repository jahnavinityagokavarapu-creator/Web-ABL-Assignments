FLAVOURS OF CAKES

A bakery is a warm and inviting establishment that produces and sells flour-based goods baked using dry heat in an oven, such as fresh bread, rolls, cookies, pastries, pies, and custom-designed cakes. Many local and artisan bakeries also function as cozy cafés where customers can sit down to enjoy hot coffee or tea alongside their treats.

Sensory Details Sights: Glass display cases filled with golden loaves, flaky croissants, and colorful cupcakes, alongside chalkboards, stainless steel counters, and handwritten price cards.
Smells: The warm, comforting aroma of rising yeast, melted butter, fresh coffee, and baking spices.
Sounds: The hum of industrial mixers, the ring of the cash register, the crackle of brown paper bags, and quiet morning chatter.
Textures and Tastes: Powdery sugar, sticky glazes, warm crusts, and soft, rich interiors.
